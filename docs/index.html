    How to play

    Roll six dice up to three times per turn. Keep any dice between rolls, then score one open category. You can score early. A score of zero scratches that category.

    Upper section
    Add the dice showing that face. A total of 84 or more earns a 50-point bonus.
    Pairs, groups and full house
    Separate groups use different faces. Larger groups can supply smaller groups, and only the required dice count. A full house is three of one face and two of another.
    Straights and Chance
    Low straight (1–5) scores 15, high straight (2–6) scores 20, and full straight (1–6) scores 30. Chance adds all six dice.
    Super-Yatzy
    Six matching dice score 100 plus their sum. Each category is used once. There are no extra Yatzy bonuses or carried-over rolls.
    Physical dice
    Count your points, choose an open category, and enter the score. The game checks whether that score is possible in that category. The highest total after 18 rounds wins. Tied players share the win.
